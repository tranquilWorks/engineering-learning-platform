# Controls/GNC conversion authoring contract

Each retained item is converted in source order and remains invisible until its item gate passes. A native item contains `module.yaml`, an expounded `lesson.md`, a deterministic `experiment.py`, an attested `conversion.yaml`, and two independent numeric evidence pairs.

Every experiment keeps equations ahead of shortcuts, exposes at least two bounded numeric levers, reproduces the source failure with `broken_mode`, and states an honest recovery. P24 is a software-only virtual plant/protocol exercise; physical HIL, browser, accessibility, learner-effectiveness, deployment, and production claims require separate evidence.
