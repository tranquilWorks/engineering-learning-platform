## Claim boundary and residual risk

This evidence establishes one catalog-visible, deterministic, source-bound
24-lesson Controls/GNC software course. The Python expected/actual numeric
paths are distinct and within declared tolerances. A licensed MATLAB runtime
was not available, so MATLAB parity remains `not_run`.

Browser visual review, accessibility acceptance, learner effectiveness,
hostile-code isolation, physical HIL/HWIL and timing, bench/field behavior,
security/release review, deployment, and production operation were not
performed and are not claimed. In particular, P24 simulates a virtual
controller/plant protocol with latency, packet loss, watchdog, and safe-zero
behavior; it is not evidence of physical hardware execution.
