## Outcome

P49-P53 implement the reviewed estimation and SLAM slice as five distinct Python-first numerical laboratories. The checkpoint contains 53 implemented Robotics modules and 207 interactive modules across five courses. P54-P69 remain: advanced planning, manipulation, system integration, and two cumulative capstones.

| Item | Executable model | Named failure and recovery | Primary retained evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P49 | Four-parameter asynchronous inertial/position information matrix with timestamped sensitivity rows, rank, conditioning, and normalized covariance | Zero commanded excitation deletes the accelerometer-scale column; restore persistent excitation and real fast/slow timestamps | Baseline rank 4; broken rank 3 with singular information |
| P50 | Two-dimensional whitened innovations, squared Mahalanobis gate, and post-gate Huber loss | Bypass the gate and apply unbounded quadratic loss; restore gate-before-robust ordering | Baseline accepts the inlier and rejects the outlier; broken accepted cost rises from about 0.26 to 68.17 |
| P51 | Iterated two-dimensional Gauss-Newton pose graph with odometry, loop, prior, and nonlinear landmark-range factors | Remove absolute/range factors, offset the graph, and linearize once; restore an explicit gauge prior and repeated relinearization | Baseline trajectory RMSE about 0.0122 m; broken RMSE about 0.549 m with singular normal matrix |
| P52 | Appearance proposal, independent geometric gate, Huber influence, and map-deformation surrogate | Insert a high-score aliased place without geometry and use quadratic influence; restore two-stage verification and bounded factor influence | Baseline deformation 0.01872 m; false closure deformation 3.42 m |
| P53 | Constant-velocity Kalman filter, Rauch-Tung-Striebel backward pass, covariance recursion, NEES coverage, and withheld position-fix audit | Publish the causal filter as a smoother and shrink covariance independently; restore coupled state/covariance smoothing and held-out audit | Baseline RMSE about 0.0348 m and 96.3% two-sigma coverage; broken RMSE about 0.0565 m and 43.2% coverage |

Each lesson is topic-specific and between 1,284 and 1,399 words. Every item retains governing derivations, conventions, domain quantities and units, a baseline, two one-variable sweeps, limiting cases, a falsifying broken case, exact recovery, an engineering review checklist, and a focused teach-back. The new reference functions independently assemble or recur through the reviewed equations; they import no production experiment, consume no production result, and perturb no production value. Production and expected evidence remain separate for all five scenarios.
